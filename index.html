About The Project

About Campus Saathi

"Find Your Way. Feel Safer. Speak Up."

Our Mission

Campus Saathi was born from a fundamental belief: every university student deserves to feel safe, confident, and oriented anywhere on campus, at any hour of the day or night. Far too often, navigation apps treat university campuses as blank spaces, while safety reporting mechanisms remain fragmented, intimidated by bureaucracy, or neglected.

Key Architecture Pillars

Building-to-Building Navigation

Leaflet.js and OpenStreetMap routing with real distance meters and walking times along actual university walkways.

Well-Lit Night Corridors

Real-time illuminated path prioritization ensures students walking at night stay within verified CCTV and security coverage.

Emergency SOS Synthesis

2-step verification, one-touch call connection, and browser-based loud audio siren synthesis to deter threats.

Statutory Anti-Ragging

UGC and Supreme Court compliant whistleblower channel with anonymous submission and docket tracking.

Privacy & Data Ethics

Campus Saathi never sells, monetizes, or shares user telemetry. GPS coordinates are only read upon explicit user request and never tracked in the background. Anonymous whistleblowers remain strictly protected.
